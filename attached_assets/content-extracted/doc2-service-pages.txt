Forestry Mulching: The Efficient Alternative
For many sites, forestry mulching is the most efficient and cost-effective clearing method. A single machine grinds trees, brush, and stumps directly into mulch that stays on site — protecting the soil, reducing erosion, and eliminating the cost and mess of hauling debris. The mulch layer also decomposes naturally, improving soil health over time.
Forestry mulching is ideal for acreage where the goal is vegetation control rather than construction grading — pasture expansion, fence line management, and perimeter clearing all benefit from this method.

Grading for Drainage
More than 80% of land drainage problems have their root in improper grade. Water follows the slope of the land — when that slope is wrong, water pools where it shouldn&apos;t, runs where it causes damage, and saturates soil that should stay dry. P1 grades with drainage as the primary outcome: every slope, every swale, every transition is designed to move water where it belongs.

Grading for Turf Establishment
Turf installation on improperly graded ground fails. Before P1 lays sod or seeds an acre, we verify the grade is correct for drainage and root establishment. When we handle both grading and turf, you get a result that lasts — and a single point of accountability if it doesn&apos;t.

Equipment & Precision
P1 uses GPS-guided grading equipment where precision tolerances demand it, and experienced operators for the full range of work from rough shaping to fine finish. We work on properties from a few acres to several hundred, across both commercial and agricultural applications.
