Executive Summary
P1 Land & Property Management provides comprehensive land and property care for commercial, industrial, agricultural, and large residential properties (1 acre and larger) across two high-growth markets: Upstate South Carolina (Greenville/Spartanburg corridor) and the greater Charlotte, North Carolina region.

This document provides a complete website project plan including:
Competitive landscape analysis for both markets
Recommended website structure and sitemap
SEO and keyword strategy targeting high-intent commercial buyers
Phased development timeline with milestones

The objective is a professional, lead-generating website that positions P1 as the go-to contractor for large-acreage property work — from weekly maintenance contracts to full property reconstruction projects. The website must clearly differentiate P1 from small residential lawn services and attract commercial, agricultural, and industrial clients.

Upstate SC Market Opportunity
No single competitor in Upstate SC clearly owns the full-service large-acreage positioning. Most companies are either earthworks specialists (clearing/grading) or landscaping/maintenance companies. P1’s combination of weekly maintenance through full property reconstruction — including drainage, pond management, turf, and trees on properties 1 acre and larger — is a genuine differentiator with no direct competition currently marketing it cohesively online.

Charlotte Market Opportunity
The Charlotte market is larger and more competitive than Upstate SC, but the same gap exists: no company is marketing a cohesive “full property lifecycle” service — from regular maintenance to large-scale reconstruction — for commercial and agricultural landowners with 1+ acre properties. The Lake Norman and greater Mecklenburg County area have strong demand from high-value rural and semi-rural property owners who need exactly what P1 offers.

Competitive Summary & P1 Positioning
Based on the analysis above, P1’s competitive position should center on three core differentiators:

Full-service, single-vendor property management — from weekly turf maintenance to complete property reconstruction, drainage, land clearing, grading, pond and waterway management. No competitor currently markets this end-to-end offering.
Large-acreage commercial, industrial, and agricultural focus — P1 deliberately targets properties 1 acre and larger, which signals expertise and deters the low-margin small-residential competition that clogs most competitors’ pipelines.
Dual-market coverage — Serving both Upstate SC and the Charlotte, NC region gives P1 scale and credibility that most local operators cannot claim.

The website must lead with these differentiators on every page. The homepage, service pages, and contact forms should all reinforce that P1 is not a lawn service — it is a full-scope land and property management contractor for serious landowners.

On-Page SEO Priorities
Each service page must have a unique H1 containing the primary keyword + geographic modifier
Meta titles: under 60 characters; format: [Service] [City/Region] | P1 Land & Property Management
Meta descriptions: under 155 characters; include the primary keyword and a call to action
Image alt text: descriptive and keyword-rich (e.g., “land clearing Greenville SC commercial property”)
Schema markup: LocalBusiness, Service, and Review schema on all key pages
Google Business Profile: separate profiles for Upstate SC and Charlotte NC markets

Local SEO Strategy
Create two Google Business Profile listings — one for each market
Build service-area pages for sub-markets: Greenville, Spartanburg, Anderson, Gaffney (SC); Charlotte, Concord, Mooresville, Lake Norman, Gastonia (NC)
Acquire citations in Angi, HomeAdvisor, Houzz, and regional agricultural/commercial directories
Pursue reviews from commercial and agricultural clients on Google; embed on Testimonials page
Target local backlinks: SC Farm Bureau, Greater Greenville Chamber, Lake Norman Chamber, Charlotte Regional Business Alliance

Content Marketing (Blog)
A consistent publishing cadence of 2–4 articles per month targeting Tier 3 keywords builds long-term organic authority and positions P1 as a trusted expert in the region. Recommended topic clusters:
Land Management (clearing, grading, drainage how-tos for large acreage)
Turf & Planting (seed selection, sodding, seasonal care for Carolinas climate)
Pond & Waterway (maintenance guides, water quality, shoreline management)
Project Spotlights (before/after case studies on commercial/agricultural projects)
Regional Guides (Upstate SC land management tips; Charlotte area seasonal property care)

Project Timeline & Phases
The recommended build is structured in four phases over approximately 16–20 weeks. Each phase has defined deliverables and sign-off checkpoints to keep the project on track and budget.

Phase
Duration
Deliverables & Tasks
Phase 1 — Discovery & Strategy
Weeks 1–2
Kick-off meeting; finalize service list and target markets; define brand voice and visual identity; compile competitor URLs and messaging audit; finalize keyword targets; agree on sitemap and page structure; set up project management and communication tools
Phase 2 — Design & Content
Weeks 3–7
Logo / brand refinement; homepage wireframe and design mockup for client approval; design all service page templates; write all page copy (homepage, 9 service pages, 2 service area pages, About, Contact); source and organize project photography; write 3 launch blog posts targeting Tier 3 keywords; develop meta titles and descriptions for all pages
Phase 3 — Development & SEO Setup
Weeks 8–13
Build site on WordPress or Webflow (TBD); implement all page designs; integrate contact / estimate form with email notifications; connect Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console; implement schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, Review); create and optimize two Google Business Profile listings; submit sitemap to Google; set up call tracking; mobile and speed optimization; cross-browser testing
Phase 4 — Launch & Post-Launch
Weeks 14–16
Final QA and client review; client sign-off; site launch; 301 redirects if replacing existing site; submit Google Business Profile posts for each market; begin citation building in top 20 directories; publish 3 launch blog posts; set up monthly SEO reporting dashboard; train client on content updates and photo uploads
Ongoing — Monthly Retainer
Monthly
2–4 blog posts per month; Google Business Profile management and posts; monthly SEO performance report (rankings, traffic, leads); quarterly keyword and competitor audit; review management and response; ongoing citation cleanup; conversion rate optimization as data accumulates

Key Milestones
Week 2: Strategy and sitemap approved
Week 5: Homepage design approved
Week 7: All copy and content delivered for review
Week 10: Development staging site ready for client review
Week 13: Final client approval of complete site
Week 14–16: Site live; Google Business Profiles active; citations submitted
Month 3: First SEO performance report with ranking baseline

Technology Recommendations
CMS: WordPress (with GeneratePress or Kadence theme) or Webflow — both support strong SEO foundations
Forms: Gravity Forms or Typeform for the estimate request form with conditional logic by service type
Analytics: Google Analytics 4 + Google Search Console; Hotjar for heatmaps post-launch
Call Tracking: CallRail to attribute phone leads to specific pages and marketing channels
SEO Plugin: Rank Math or Yoast SEO for on-page optimization workflows
Speed: WP Rocket or Perfmatters for caching; WebP images; CDN via Cloudflare
Hosting: WPEngine, Kinsta, or Cloudways for managed WordPress; Webflow hosting if Webflow is chosen
