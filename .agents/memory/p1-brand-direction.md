# P1 Land & Property Management — brand palette direction

The user decided the visual system should be:
- **Primary accent = the logo BLUE** (a confident professional blue; deep navy for dark sections). Blue leads buttons, links, dark grounds.
- **Green = supporting only** (the old brand green ~hsl(145 40% 35%)) — checkmarks/small highlights, NOT dominant.
- **Add a warm EARTH accent** — clay/terracotta/tan — for warmth (eyebrow kickers, dividers, hover states).
- Overall goal: "more rich, with texture and depth" — avoid flat solid-color blocks; use tinted/layered (navy-toward) shadows, subtle gradients, and a connective "Topographic" land motif (contour lines / soil strata).

**Why:** explicit user preferences during the design-refinement exploration (was green-primary before).
**How to apply:** when graduating any redesign or editing index.css, make blue the primary token, demote green to a supporting accent, and introduce a clay/tan accent token. Three explored directions live in `artifacts/mockup-sandbox/src/components/mockups/p1-redesign/` (Topographic = refined/subtle, Earthwork = bold/rugged, Editorial = layered/premium).
