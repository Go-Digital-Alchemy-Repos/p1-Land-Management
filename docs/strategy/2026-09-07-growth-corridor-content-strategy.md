# P1 growth-corridor content strategy

**Status:** Owner-approved strategy; public publication remains subject to evidence, technical review, customer permission, and the active CMS/public release gates.

## Positioning

P1 serves commercial and industrial exterior sites across four connected service pillars:

1. Commercial and industrial grounds management
2. Land management and clearing
3. Site work and earthwork
4. Commercial concrete flatwork (sidewalks, curbs, pads, ramps, repairs, and comparable non-structural site concrete)

The public message is: **one accountable exterior-site contractor for large commercial and industrial properties.**

## Market model

### Core markets — local search and proof priority

### Select-project markets — availability is confirmed project by project

- Chester / Richburg / I-77 / SC-9

- Kershaw / Bethune until confirmed projects and serviceability exist

Lancaster remains a core market for commercial, mixed-use, and development work, not data-center positioning.

## Evidence and publication controls

Each Project Story requires: approved approximate location; property type; service tags; scope or acreage band; pre-work constraint; factual work performed; support for every result claim; photo rights; client/publication permission where needed; technical reviewer; review date; and related service/market/estimate links.

Dashboard evidence remains private. It must not publish automatically to the CMS. A manager and editor must explicitly approve any field photo, report, note, or customer result before it becomes marketing content.

## Technical and measurement requirements

- The first release adds no public API. The CMS adds a `project_story` editorial format with market classification, service tags, evidence/permission, reviewer, review date, and approved media fields.
- Track content type, service pillar, market, CTA click, accepted request, qualified lead, site assessment, estimate, and won work. Keep contact details out of analytics.
- Validate one H1, page title, description, canonical/schema lifecycle, accessible alt text, responsive image assets, contextual estimate CTA, and lead-source attribution for every published item.
- Never create local SEO pages outside core markets without confirmed routine coverage, unique local commercial/industrial context, and approved evidence.
