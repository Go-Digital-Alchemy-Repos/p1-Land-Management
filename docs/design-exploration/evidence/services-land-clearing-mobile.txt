  - 'heading "Forestry Mulching: The Efficient Alternative" [level=2]'
  - paragraph: For many sites, forestry mulching is the most efficient and cost-effective clearing method. A single machine grinds trees, brush, and stumps directly into mulch that stays on site — protecting the soil, reducing erosion, and eliminating the cost and mess of hauling debris. The mulch layer also decomposes naturally, improving soil health over time.
  - paragraph: Forestry mulching is ideal for acreage where the goal is vegetation control rather than construction grading — pasture expansion, fence line management, and perimeter clearing all benefit from this method.
