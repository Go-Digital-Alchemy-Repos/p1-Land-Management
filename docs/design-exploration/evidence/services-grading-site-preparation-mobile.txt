    - generic: Pad preparation — building pads for structures, equipment storage, or hardscapes

  - heading "Grading for Drainage" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Water follows the slope of the land. When that slope sends runoff toward a building or low spot, regrading may be part of the repair. We consider water movement when shaping slopes, swales, and transitions.
  - img "Graded swale channeling water across a property"
  - heading "Grading for Turf Establishment" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Good turf starts with well-prepared ground. We check the grade, drainage, and soil before planting, then explain the watering and care needed while the grass takes root.
  - img "Freshly graded soil transitioning into new sod"
  - heading "Grading for New Construction" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Before foundations, pavement, or utilities go in, the ground needs to match the approved plans. We prepare pads, slopes, drainage routes, and sub-grade with your project team.
