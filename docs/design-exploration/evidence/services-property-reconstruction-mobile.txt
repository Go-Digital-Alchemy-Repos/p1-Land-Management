  - heading "What Full Property Reconstruction Includes" [level=2]
  - paragraph: "The work depends on your property. A reconstruction project may include:"
  - list:
    - generic: Complete land clearing — removal of all unwanted vegetation, trees, stumps, and debris
    - generic: Rough grading — reshaping the topography to proper drainage slope
    - generic: Drainage system installation — French drains, swales, ditches, and retention systems
    - generic: Erosion control — slope stabilization, rip-rap, silt fencing, and matting
    - generic: Fine grading — precision finish grade for turf, drainage, and structural stability
    - generic: Pond restoration or reconstruction — dredging, embankment repair, inlet/outlet work
    - generic: Waterway clearing and bank stabilization
    - generic: Soil amendment — lime, fertilizer, and soil conditioning for turf establishment
    - generic: Seeding or sod installation — large-acreage turf establishment appropriate to use
    - generic: Transition to ongoing maintenance program
